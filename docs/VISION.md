## What Unison is

**Unison provides a leadership view of the team's work.** It helps people understand what the work is meant to enable, what value the evidence demonstrates, and where their attention or judgment can change an outcome. People lead without inspecting every activity or becoming the approval point for ordinary work.

Team Pulse supplies shared team context and evidence. Unison turns that material into an experience for understanding, probing, deciding and following the response. It connects work to the team's stated direction, including disagreements and unclear alignment. It does not invent agreement about priorities.

**Portfolio** shows the landscape before personal filtering: intended outcomes, capabilities, beneficiaries, ownership, dependencies and evidence gaps. **For you** selects what changes understanding or makes the person's participation useful. Both views use the same saved context, and each item leads to its Portfolio work. Relevance is not automatically urgency, obligation or authority.

The person moves from a concise claim to its sources, tests, reviews, counterevidence and actual work. They ask, connect, decide, delegate, defer or deliberately take no action. A later visit shows the response, returned evidence and unresolved questions without reconstructing the context.

**Understand → Probe → Judge or delegate → Observe the response → Reassess**

## One product, three connected perspectives

Leadership is a perspective on responsibility, not an executive title or a product reserved for named people.

| Perspective | Scope | Useful attention |
|---|---|---|
| **Effort** | One initiative or capability: intended outcome, beneficiaries, demonstrated value, dependencies and missing proof. | A question, contribution or decision that can move that work. |
| **Area** | Related efforts and capabilities: how they fit together, what they enable, and dependencies across the area's boundaries. | Area-level trade-offs, shared capabilities and coordination with other owners. |
| **Whole team** | Outcomes and relationships across all areas, even without direct ownership of an effort. | Cross-area opportunities, gaps, sponsorship and team-level judgment; local work stays with its owners. |

Samuel's Team Pulse area and a skip-level manager's whole-team view illustrate these perspectives, not fixed personas. A person can widen or narrow a clear, correctable focus without losing team context. Perspective changes explanation and attention—not facts, source access or permission to act.

## Five leadership questions

1. **What is this work meant to enable, for whom—and what value is demonstrated?** Distinguish intended capability, delivery, use and benefit. Identify the next missing proof.
2. **What materially changed, what did we learn, and how is outcome confidence changing?** Compare with a stated baseline. Explain verification, competing explanations and what remains unknown.
3. **Which opportunities, challenges or trade-offs could change outcomes across the portfolio?** Explain affected outcomes, dependencies and gaps—not every local update.
4. **Where would my attention or judgment help—and what should remain with others?** Explain why now, options, consequences, authority and accountable ownership.
5. **What followed a decision or response—and what remains unresolved?** Show the response, learning, returned evidence and next check. “After” does not establish “because of.”

## Principles

1. **Value is more than activity.** Investment → lasting capability → evidence of use → learning → next decision. Shared infrastructure and failed experiments can matter. Delivery, distribution, adoption, reliability and benefit remain separate claims.
2. **Confidence is earned, not styled.** Sources, verification and contrary evidence support consequential claims. A citation or fresh document does not prove delivery, benefit or causality. Missing evidence is not zero.
3. **Attention has a reason.** A quiet For you distinguishes no qualifying attention found in the evidenced scope from insufficient context to judge. An unchanged suggestion does not repeatedly demand action.
4. **People retain judgment and authority.** Recommendations are not decisions. Ownership and permissions are explicit; routine work stays with its owners. Generated content does not authorize execution or shared writes.
5. **The experience is quiet, not opaque.** Plain language, clear hierarchy and contextual inspection keep evidence near the claim. Keyboard use, narrow screens, themes and reduced motion work without hiding uncertainty or recovery.
6. **Returning does not restart the work.** Saved results, decisions and request history retain their identities. Opening retained material does not generate. Recovery checks the original uncertain request before any retry or replacement; a new request name does not resolve an unknown outcome.
7. **Operation supports the leadership task.** Library, CLI, standalone web and compatible MCP App entry points share meaning. People can identify effective connections, AI routes and storage, use their own identity, and preserve work through supported backup, upgrade and removal. Configuration records authority; it does not create it.

## What Unison deliberately resists

- Activity feeds, task-board replacement, surveillance and people or productivity rankings.
- Treating spend, activity, distribution or adoption as proof of value, ROI or outcome confidence.
- Replacing Team Pulse, its source systems, the host's controls or execution services.
- Making every update a review request, or requiring manual Portfolio assembly or mandatory initial draft approval before use.
- Implicit promises of complete source coverage, arbitrary servers, offline protected access, cross-device sync or unsupported platforms.

## How you can tell it is useful

- An effort or area lead explains intended and demonstrated value, missing proof and cross-area dependencies; a whole-team reader relates those efforts to team direction.
- A reader distinguishes supported confidence from unknowns, knows whether their participation helps, and can leave ordinary work with another owner.
- A returning reader sees what followed a response, preserves prior context and can recover an interrupted request without duplicated work.

Success is better-informed judgment, useful delegation and visible learning—not more cards or interruptions. These signs guide verification; they are not claims of measured benefit.
